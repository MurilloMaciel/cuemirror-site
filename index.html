How it works

Three steps, then you're recording.

  1. 01

    Write or paste

    Type your script in the app, paste it from anywhere, or dictate it with your keyboard's microphone.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Set the speed, text size, margins and colours to match the way you read.

  3. 03

    Read to the camera

    Press play, wait for the countdown and read with your eyes near the lens. Adjust as you go.
