How it works

Three steps, then you're recording.

  1. 01

    Write or paste

    Type your script in the app, paste it from anywhere, or dictate it with your keyboard's microphone.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Set the speed, text size, margins and colors to match the way you read.

  3. 03

    Read to the camera

    Press play, wait for the countdown and read with your eyes near the lens. Adjust as you go, or record the take right in the app.

Highlights

More than scrolling text.

  • PREMIUM

    Record while you read

    Film yourself with the front camera while the script scrolls at the top of the screen, close to the lens. Watch the take, then save it to your gallery or share it.

  • PREMIUM

    Hands-free with Bluetooth

    With a Bluetooth keyboard or pedal: press to play or pause, hold to go back to the start.

  • PREMIUM

    Ready for teleprompter glass

    Mirrored and Flipped modes for rigs with a glass, so the text reads the right way in the reflection.

  • PREMIUM

    Bring your script

    Import .txt, .docx and .pdf files, including from Google Docs, and export any script as .txt.
