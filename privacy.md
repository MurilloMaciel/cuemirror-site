# Privacy Policy — CueMirror

**English** · [Português](privacy-pt.html) · [Español](privacy-es.html) · [Français](privacy-fr.html) · [Deutsch](privacy-de.html) · [Italiano](privacy-it.html) · [Bahasa Indonesia](privacy-id.html)

Last updated: November 16, 2026

CueMirror is a teleprompter app for Android and iOS. It requires no account, does not sync data to the cloud, and shows no ads.

Data controller: EmQube (EMQUBE TECNOLOGIA E SERVICOS LTDA, CNPJ 47.035.256/0001-06), São Leopoldo, RS, Brazil. Contact: support@cuemirror.com.

### Your scripts, recordings and settings

Everything you create in CueMirror stays on your device. CueMirror has no servers of its own and never sends your scripts or your videos to us or to any third party.

- **Scripts and settings** (theme, colors, reading speed and the like) are stored in the app's local storage.
- **Videos you record in the app** are stored in the app's private storage until you save or discard them on the review screen. If you tap **Save to gallery**, a copy goes to your device's photo library (on Android, in the "Movies/CueMirror" folder). Once the video is saved or discarded, the app deletes its private copy.
- **Exported scripts** (.txt) are written to the app's temporary cache only so the system share sheet can hand them to the app you choose. The file is deleted when you export another script, or by the system when it clears the cache.

If system backup is turned on for your device (Google Backup on Android, iCloud on iOS), the operating system may include your scripts and settings in your account's backup. That backup is made and managed by Google or Apple, and we have no access to it. Videos waiting for review, exported files and the local subscription record are excluded from backups. Uninstalling the app deletes its local data, including any video you have not saved yet; when you reinstall, the system may restore scripts and settings from that backup.

Some settings are included, in summarized form, in the usage data described under "Automatically collected data", and only if sharing is turned on.

### Camera, microphone and photo library

CueMirror asks for camera and microphone access only when you open **Record with your script**, a Premium feature that records you with the front camera while you read. The video and the audio are captured and stored on your device only; CueMirror does not send them anywhere and does not analyze them. You can deny or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

On iOS, CueMirror asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you tap **Save to gallery**, and it can only add videos, not read your library. On Android, saving to the gallery needs no permission.

When you tap **Share** on a recording or **Export as .txt** on a script, the system share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes. From then on, the file is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own policy.

### Other features that work on your device

- **Importing files:** to import a script (.txt, .docx or .pdf), the app opens the system file picker and reads only the file you choose. The text is extracted on your device. If you pick a Google Docs document on Android, Google Drive converts it to a file before handing it to the app.
- **Dictation:** voice dictation uses your device's keyboard. CueMirror does not record or receive that audio; it is handled by your keyboard's provider (for example, Google or Apple) under its own policy.
- **Remote control:** a Bluetooth keyboard or pedal is paired in your device's settings. CueMirror only reacts to the keys pressed while you read, and needs no Bluetooth or location permission.
- **Floating window (Android):** the picture-in-picture window shows your script over other apps and sends nothing anywhere.
- **Rating the app:** from time to time, after a few completed readings, the app may ask whether you want to rate it. If you tap Rate, the review is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; CueMirror is not told whether you rated or what you wrote.
- **Links:** the About screen opens our website, your email app (to write to support) and the store page. Those are handled by your browser, email app or store.

### Automatically collected data

To identify crashes, understand how the app is used, and be able to remotely turn off a feature that malfunctions, CueMirror uses Google Firebase services:

- **Firebase Crashlytics**: when the app crashes, or when it recovers from an unexpected error, it sends a technical report with information such as where in the code the error occurred, the kind of error, device model, operating system version and app version.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase**: records usage events, such as screens opened and features used, events that Firebase records automatically, such as first open, sessions, time spent in the app and app updates, and technical device data such as device model, operating system version, language and app version. On Android, it also records the install source reported by Google Play (for example, whether the app was installed from a link or a campaign).
- **Firebase Remote Config**: lets us remotely turn off an app feature that is malfunctioning, without publishing a new version. When the app opens and when you return to it, CueMirror fetches the current settings, sending the platform, the app version and an installation identifier. It sends no usage events and none of your content.

Usage events may include:

- creating, duplicating, deleting, importing or exporting a script, and starting, finishing, pausing, restarting or leaving a reading session;
- reading and app settings, such as speed, text size, margin, mirror mode, alignment, line spacing, theme and whether auto-save is on;
- the approximate size of a script and of your library, always in ranges (for example, "100 to 499 words" or "5 to 19 scripts"), never the text;
- the format of an imported file (.txt, .docx or .pdf) and whether the import succeeded;
- whether a search found results, never the search term;
- for in-app recording: that a recording started, the video quality the camera used, the length of the take in ranges, why it ended (for example, you stopped it or it was interrupted) and whether you saved or discarded it;
- the use of the floating window and of a remote control (only the kind of key, for example "volume" or "media");
- whether the rating request was shown and whether you chose to rate or dismissed it;
- the progress of a purchase or a subscription restore (the plan chosen and the outcome);
- when the app recovers from an error: which operation failed and the technical type of the error, never its message.

These services associate the data with identifiers generated by Firebase for each app installation and, on iOS, with the identifier the system assigns to apps from the same developer (IDFV). They do not identify you by name, email or account. CueMirror does not use the device's advertising ID. Google uses the IP address of the request to estimate approximate location (country, region and city) and then discards the full IP address. The app does not access GPS or request location permission.

**The content of your scripts and recordings is never included in this data.** This applies to text, titles, the names of imported or exported files, search terms, video and audio.

The data is transmitted encrypted and handled by Google, as our service provider, according to its own policy, and may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States:

- [Firebase Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We use this data only to fix bugs and improve the app, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving its features (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); Brazil's LGPD, Art. 7, IX). We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising and do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

### How to turn off data collection

Sending this data is on by default. You can turn it off at any time under **Settings → Privacy → Share usage and crash data**. With the option off, the app stops sending usage events and crash reports. Your choice is saved on your device and stays in effect until you change it. The Firebase Remote Config check continues even with the option off, because it keeps the app working safely and sends no usage data.

### Purchases and subscriptions

CueMirror offers an optional Premium plan as a subscription (monthly or yearly). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store: CueMirror has no access to payment data, requires no account and does not operate its own servers.

To know whether you have an active subscription, the app queries the store on your device (for example, when the app opens and when you tap **Settings → Restore subscription**). The result is stored only in your device's local storage so the app works offline, and it is never included in backups. We do not receive your name, email or store account identifier.

Google Analytics for Firebase records purchase events (product, price and currency) and the progress of a purchase in the app (the plan chosen and whether the purchase was left pending, canceled or failed), without any payment data. Cancellation, plan changes and refunds are handled in the store. See also the [Terms of Use](terms.html?lang=en).

### Permissions

- **Camera and microphone:** only for in-app recording, as described above.
- **Add to photo library (iOS):** only when you save a recording.
- CueMirror does not request location, contacts, Bluetooth or storage permission, and does not read your photo library.

### Your rights

The technical data collected is not linked to any account, name or email, so we usually cannot identify which data belongs to you. Crash reports are automatically deleted within 90 days, and individual usage events within 2 months. Google may keep aggregated reports that do not identify installations. You can object to this collection at any time by turning off the option described in "How to turn off data collection", or stop it entirely by uninstalling the app. The scripts and videos on your device are under your control: you can delete them in the app at any time.

For questions or to exercise your rights under applicable data protection laws (such as the LGPD or the GDPR), including access, correction, deletion and objection, write to us at the email below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

### Children

CueMirror is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.
